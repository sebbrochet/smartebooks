# 1. Welcome to the sampler

This second book exists to prove the **platform** hosts more than one title from the
**same engine**. Everything interactive here is the same machinery as the guide — no
architecture was copied.

> 📌 **Key concept**: A book is just content + config. The engine (pipeline, islands,
> store, reader) is shared, so evolving it improves every book at once.

## A quick check
